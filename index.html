01 / OVERVIEW

New perspectives.
Shared possibilities.

An international gathering to advance interactive intelligence through academic exchange and collaboration.

Hosted by the Interactive Intelligence Lab (IIL) at the Institute of Science Tokyo and sponsored by Alaya Lab, the symposium brings researchers together to share their work and explore opportunities for joint research.

The program begins with an excursion and networking day, followed by lab introductions, research presentations, and four invited keynotes.
